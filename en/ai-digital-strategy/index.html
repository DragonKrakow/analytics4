Frequently asked questions

1. Does AI replace marketing strategy?

No. AI speeds up testing, segmentation and reporting. Strategy — goals, KPIs, priorities — stays with people and the partner.

2. Which use cases should we start with?

Highest-ROI ones: audience on first-party data, creative variants, demand/budget prediction and automated anomaly alerts.

3. Do we need a large AI budget?

Not at the start. Many use cases run on tools you already use (Ads, GA4, CRM) and prompts. We scale after proving the effect.

4. How do you measure AI impact?

Before/after KPIs: creative production time, CPA/ROAS, lead quality, reporting time. Without measurement we do not scale.

5. Is AI only about Google Ads?

No. Ads is one area. We also work on audience, landings, reports and marketing process automation.

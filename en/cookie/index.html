Cookie Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This site uses cookies and similar technologies. Details below.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They enable site functionality, analytics and (with consent) ad personalisation.

2. Types we use

  • Necessary – required for the site to work (no consent needed).
  • Analytics – Google Analytics 4 with Consent Mode V2. Activated only with your consent.
  • Marketing – Google Ads (remarketing, conversions). Only with consent.

3. Consent Mode V2

We use Google Consent Mode V2. Default state for EEA users is “denied". After interaction with the consent banner we update the signals (ad_storage, analytics_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization).

4. Managing consent

You can change or withdraw consent at any time via browser settings or the cookie banner. Refusing analytics/marketing cookies does not affect essential site functionality.

Frequently asked questions

1. How much does Google Ads management cost?

Pricing is tailored: fixed fee, % of media spend, or performance-based. After the account audit we propose scope and 90-day KPIs.

2. Do you work with Performance Max and Shopping?

Yes. PMax and Shopping are standard for e-commerce. We control the Merchant Center feed, conversion signals and structure so automation does not run blind.

3. How soon will I see results?

The first 2–4 weeks cover audit, tracking and structure rebuild. Stable ROAS/CPA signals usually appear after 30–60 days of continuous optimisation, depending on budget and vertical.

4. Do I need my own ad budget?

Yes — media spend is on the client side. We manage the account, strategy and optimisation. A sensible minimum budget depends on industry and competition.

5. What does a Google Ads audit include?

Account structure, conversion tracking, feed quality, bidding settings, campaign overlap and reporting. Deliverable: action plan and 90-day KPIs.

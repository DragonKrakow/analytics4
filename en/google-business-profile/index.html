Google Maps & Local SEO: how to increase your business visibility on Google

Local SEO, Google Maps, Google Business Profile, Local Pack and reviews — get found by customers in your area.

In short (30 seconds)
  • Problem: people looking for a nearby service open Maps and compare businesses — if you are not visible, you do not exist.
  • Tool: Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the free listing on Search and Maps.
  • Ranking: relevance, distance and prominence; the Local Pack shows featured businesses.
  • Levers: complete profile, authentic reviews, consistent NAP data and geographic monitoring.
  • Goal: turn local visibility into calls, directions and sales opportunities.

When someone searches for a restaurant, a shop, a professional or a nearby service, they rarely browse dozens of websites: they open Google Maps, compare businesses and choose the one that builds the most trust. Being visible in Google's local results means intercepting potential customers at the exact moment they are looking for what you offer.

Local SEO is the set of optimisation activities that help a business improve visibility in geographically relevant searches on Google Search and Google Maps. It is not only about website ranking, but also profile quality, reviews, data consistency and online reputation.

Google Business Profile: your digital storefront

Google Business Profile, previously known as Google My Business, is the free tool to manage a business presence on Google. A complete listing includes address, phone, opening hours, services, photos, website and reviews.

Professional management improves information relevance, makes it easier for customers to get in touch and strengthens presence in local search. Updating hours, choosing the right categories, listing accurate services and replying to reviews are essential to keep a reliable presence.

Ranking on Google Maps and the Local Pack

Ranking on Google Maps depends mainly on three factors: relevance, distance and prominence. Google evaluates how well a business matches the query, how close it is to the user and how well known it is online and offline.

The Local Pack is the box that shows selected local businesses directly in search results, usually with a map. Appearing there can increase calls, website visits and direction requests.

Profile optimisation, local information on the website, consistent business citations and reviews help build a more competitive local presence. There are no guaranteed positions: results change with the user's location and competition.

Google reviews and local reputation

Google reviews influence both how the business is perceived and its prominence in local results. A steady process of collecting authentic feedback, with professional and timely replies, builds trust and sets you apart from competitors.

A measurable Local SEO strategy

An effective strategy starts with competitor analysis, continues with profile and website optimisation, and develops through monitoring geographic positions, calls, clicks and direction requests.

The goal is not only to appear on Google, but to turn local visibility into contacts and concrete commercial opportunities.

FAQ: Google Maps, Google Business Profile and Local SEO

1. Local SEO — general questions

1. What is Local SEO?

Local SEO is the set of SEO strategies that help a business appear in search results relevant to a specific geographic area. It includes optimising the Google Business Profile, website, reviews and online business information.

2. What is local SEO for?

It makes a business more visible to people searching for products or services in its area, increasing visits, calls, quote requests and sales contacts.

3. What is the difference between traditional SEO and Local SEO?

Traditional SEO improves organic rankings for many types of queries. Local SEO focuses on searches with geographic intent, emphasising location, service area and presence on Google Maps.

4. Which businesses need Local SEO?

Shops, restaurants, hotels, professional practices, clinics, gyms, tradespeople and service businesses — especially those that acquire customers in a given city or region.

5. How long does it take to see Local SEO results?

It depends on competition, profile quality, reputation and starting point. Some improvements can appear in a few weeks; stable visibility often takes several months.

2. Google Business Profile and Google My Business

6. What is Google Business Profile?

Google's free tool that lets eligible businesses manage their information in Google Search and Google Maps, making it easier for customers to discover and contact them.

7. Does Google My Business still exist?

The name was replaced by Google Business Profile. Many people still use the old name, so both keywords can be useful in SEO content.

8. How do you create a Google Business Profile?

Sign in with a Google account, enter real business data and complete the required verification. Follow Google's guidelines and use accurate information.

9. Is Google Business Profile free?

Yes — creating and managing a profile directly is free. Cost only comes from professional management, consulting or optimisation.

10. What information should go on the Google listing?

Real business name, correct category, address or service area, contact details, hours, website and services. Up-to-date photos improve the user experience.

11. How do you optimise a Google Business Profile?

Choose appropriate categories, complete available fields, keep services and hours current, add authentic photos and manage reviews over time. The listing must faithfully represent the business.

12. Can I manage Google Business Profile without a website?

Yes. An eligible business can have a listing without a website. A well-structured site can still strengthen local presence with content, consistent contact details and pages for services and areas served.

13. Can I have a Google listing if I work from home?

It depends on the type of business and how customers are served. Businesses that serve customers at their premises can often set a service area and hide the residential address when Google's rules allow it. Not every home-based activity is eligible.

14. How do you manage multiple locations with Google Business Profile?

Each eligible location needs accurate, specific information. Keep categories, addresses, hours and contacts consistent, and avoid duplicate listings or virtual locations that breach the guidelines.

15. Why was my Google listing suspended?

Possible causes: guideline violations, misleading information, non-compliant names, ineligible locations or verification issues. Identify the cause, fix any violations and follow the official reinstatement process.

3. Ranking on Google Maps and the Local Pack

16. How does ranking on Google Maps work?

Google ranks local businesses mainly by relevance, distance and prominence. An accurate profile, solid reputation and consistent information improve the chance of appearing for relevant searches.

17. How do you appear in the top Google Maps results?

Optimise the profile, choose the right categories, manage reviews, keep business data up to date and strengthen local signals on the website. Results also depend on the user's location and competition.

18. What is the Google Local Pack?

The local results box Google shows for certain searches, often with a map and three featured businesses. It lets users quickly compare relevant companies.

19. How do you get into the Local Pack?

Improve profile relevance, business prominence and consistency of local information. Distance remains decisive: there is no technique that guarantees entry in every area.

20. Why does my business only appear on Maps in some areas?

Google factors in the distance between the business and where the search is made. Visibility can drop as you move away from the location, even with a well-optimised profile.

21. How do you check local ranking on Google Maps?

Targeted searches and geographic grid-tracking tools show how ranking changes across different points in the area — more reliable than a single search.

22. Can I rank on Google Maps in a city where I have no location?

Visibility in a distant city cannot be guaranteed. Businesses that truly serve other areas can indicate a service area if eligible and create useful local website content. Do not invent locations or addresses to manipulate results.

23. What is the difference between Google Maps and the Local Pack?

Maps shows local results in the map experience; the Local Pack shows a selection of businesses in Google Search. The two environments are linked but display and exploration options differ.

24. Why does a competitor rank above me on Google Maps?

They may have higher relevance for the query, a closer location to the user or greater prominence. Analysing categories, reviews, business data and local signals helps identify competitive gaps.

25. How much does ranking on Google Maps cost?

There is no universal price. Consulting cost depends on number of locations, market competitiveness, review management and work needed on the site and profile. Creating the profile itself is free.

4. Google reviews and online reputation

26. How important are Google reviews for Local SEO?

Reviews contribute to local prominence and influence customer choice. Volume, rating and quality should be considered with other ranking factors, without treating them as absolute.

27. How many Google reviews are needed to rank better?

There is no minimum number that fits every business. It is more useful to compare volume, ratings and frequency with direct competitors and aim for steady authentic feedback.

28. How do you get more Google reviews from customers?

Invite real customers to share their experience via a link or QR code. The request must be transparent and must not involve incentives or pressure for positive reviews.

29. Do negative reviews penalise ranking on Google Maps?

A single negative review does not automatically cause a penalty. Overall rating, reputation and the business reply mainly affect how potential customers perceive you. Handle issues professionally.

30. How should you reply to negative Google reviews?

Reply calmly, acknowledge any issues and offer a channel to discuss further. Replies should be personalised, respectful and free of personal data or aggressive tone.

31. Can you buy Google reviews?

Buying reviews or posting fake feedback violates Google's rules and can lead to content removal and other restrictions. The right strategy is collecting authentic reviews from real customers.

32. Why are some Google reviews not published?

Google may filter or remove reviews it detects as spam, fake content or policy violations. Not every missing review was necessarily deleted — delays or technical issues can occur.

33. How do you remove a fake Google review?

The profile owner can report a review that violates Google's policies. Removal is not guaranteed: a negative review is not removed simply because the business disagrees with the opinion.

34. Do keywords in reviews help local SEO?

Reviews that describe services, products and experiences help Google understand relevance. It is not appropriate to suggest artificial text or force keywords on customers — feedback should freely reflect their experience.

35. How often should you collect new Google reviews?

Prefer a continuous process: invite customers after a real experience. Steady, natural collection is more sustainable than occasional campaigns aimed only at increasing volume quickly.

5. Optimisation, strategy and measurement

36. Does the Google Business Profile affect website ranking?

Profile and website are complementary parts of local presence, but they are not the same. An optimised profile helps appear in local results; the website can rank organically through content, technical quality and relevance.

37. What is NAP consistency in Local SEO?

NAP means Name, Address and Phone. Keeping these accurate and consistent across site, Google profile and relevant directories reduces ambiguity about the business identity.

38. What are local SEO citations?

Online mentions of a business's details — for example in business directories, trade associations and local portals. Quality, relevance and consistency matter more than sheer quantity.

39. Do photos help ranking on Google Maps?

Up-to-date photos make the listing more useful and help customers get to know the business before visiting. A direct ranking effect is not guaranteed: treat them mainly as a presentation and conversion tool.

40. Do Google Business Profile posts improve Local SEO?

Posts can communicate offers, updates and events to people viewing the listing. They help keep information current and encourage interaction, but frequent posting does not guarantee a direct ranking boost.

41. Do you need a dedicated page for every city for Local SEO?

It can help when the business truly serves multiple areas and can offer specific content for each. Pages must address real local needs and avoid duplicate text created only to target geographic keywords.

42. Which tools should you use to analyse Local SEO?

Google Business Profile, Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4 help monitor visibility, interactions and traffic. Specialist local rank-tracking tools analyse Maps ranking across geographic areas.

43. How do you measure Local SEO results?

Analyse impressions and visibility, calls, website clicks, direction requests, bookings and contacts generated. Monitoring local positions shows in which zones the strategy is improving presence.

44. Is Local SEO useful for businesses that also run Google Ads?

Yes. Local SEO and Google Ads can work together: the former builds organic visibility over time, while ads can intercept relevant searches immediately. Results should be measured separately and in their combined contribution.

45. Does Local SEO help with voice search?

A complete profile, accurate information and relevant web pages can help assistants find information about the business. There is no specific procedure that guarantees selection as a voice answer.

6. Advanced Local SEO and the future of search

46. When should you hire a Local SEO consultant?

When the business cannot improve visibility on its own, has many locations, faces strong competitors or needs a structured system for profiles, reviews and result measurement.

47. What is multi-location SEO?

SEO for businesses with multiple physical locations. It covers managing individual profiles, specific local pages, consistent data and performance monitoring per store or branch.

48. How do you analyse competitors on Google Maps?

Compare businesses that appear for main keywords in target zones, looking at categories, reviews, services, photos, business data and online presence. The goal is to find concrete improvement opportunities.

49. Does Local SEO matter for Google AI Overview and AI assistants?

A well-documented local presence, consistent information and useful content can help search systems understand the business. A strong Maps position does not guarantee citation in AI-generated answers.

50. What is the best strategy to improve local visibility on Google?

The strongest approach integrates Google Business Profile, website, authentic reviews, consistent business data, local content and geographic monitoring. Priorities should be set by competition and the commercial results the business wants to achieve.

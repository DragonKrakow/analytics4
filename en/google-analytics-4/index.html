Frequently asked questions

1. How is a proper GA4 setup different from the default?

Default GA4 collects basic traffic. A proper setup has a data layer, e-commerce/lead events, Consent Mode v2, enhanced conversions and CRM validation — data that supports bidding and decisions.

2. Do you implement Consent Mode v2?

Yes. Consent Mode v2 is the GDPR standard: conversion modelling without consent, without losing signal quality for Ads.

3. How long does tracking implementation take?

Typical project: 1–3 weeks depending on site complexity, CMS and CRM integration. After go-live we validate DebugView and CRM comparison.

4. Do you work with GTM and BigQuery?

Yes. GTM is the default tag layer; BigQuery is used for larger volumes and custom reports (funnels, attribution, cohorts).

5. What does a tracking audit include?

Event map, gap between Ads and CRM, Consent Mode, enhanced conversions and bidding signal quality. Output: priority list and fix plan.
